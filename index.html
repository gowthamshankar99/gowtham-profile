About

I am an AI/ML Solutions Architect at AWS, with 16 years of experience in IT — including 4 years at AWS and earlier work at The Hartford. I hold a Master's degree from Boston University (Metropolitan College) and am based in Connecticut.

My research interests include context-aware content safety, dynamic prompt orchestration, the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and agentic commerce.
